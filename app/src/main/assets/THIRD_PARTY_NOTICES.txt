## Device-interface research

The vendor-control route in NightKeys 0.9.5 is based on publicly documented interface behavior from **titan2-keylight** (`furkan-bayrak/titan2-keylight`, MIT) and **PhysiBoard** (`brobata/physiboard`). NightKeys does not redistribute either project's implementation. It reimplements the documented `agui_common` calls, timeout semantics and close-light broadcast in Java over an explicitly authorized Shizuku shell transport. See `docs/SOURCES.md`.

`titan2-keylight` is MIT licensed. Its repository states that it was tested on the Titan 2 Elite and documents the `agui_common` transaction interface used by the stock Agui keyboard stack.

Neither those projects, Unihertz nor Shizuku's authors endorse or validate NightKeys.
