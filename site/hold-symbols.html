Titan Tools / Hold Symbols

Hold the key.
Get the symbol.

Almost every letter on the Titan has a symbol printed on it, but typing one means reaching for Alt first. Hold Symbols lets you just hold the key instead. Tap S and you get s. Hold it for a moment and you get 4.

Tap for letters.
Hold for symbols.

Hold Symbols only steps in when you hold a plain letter in a text box. Everything else on the keyboard works the way it always has.

01 / TAP

Type like you always do.

Quick presses come out as normal letters, in the right order even when you type fast. Your keyboard app still gets every key, so autocorrect and predictions keep working.

02 / HOLD

Hold for what’s printed on it.

Keep a letter down for a little under half a second and its symbol appears, with a small tick so you know it worked. No accent menu, no row of repeated letters.

03 / THE REST

Shift and Alt don’t change.

Shift+S still gives S, and Alt+S gives whatever it gave before. Password boxes, number pads and terminals are left alone, and so is everything outside a text box.

Tick boxes are just for you to keep your place. They’re saved only in this browser and don’t control the app.

Start here: download Hold Symbols

Hold Symbols is free. It was made for the Unihertz Titan 2 Elite and its built-in keyboard.

Download Hold Symbols

The file is called TitanHoldSymbols-0.3.1.apk. “.apk” just means an Android app file.

  1. Tap Download Hold Symbols above, on your Titan 2 Elite.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open. (Missed it? Open the Files app → Downloads.)
  3. Tap Install. If your phone says it can’t install apps from this browser, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Tap Done. You’ll open it in step 2.

You should now see: a Titan Hold Symbols icon in your app drawer.

Already have an older version?

0.3.0: install straight over it. Your settings carry over.

0.2 test builds: those were signed with a different key, so your phone won’t update them. Uninstall the old one first, then install this one.

01

Start Shizuku, the helper app

Why Shizuku? When you hold a key, Hold Symbols has to type the symbol for you, the same way the system types a key. Android only allows that with Shizuku’s help. No rooting needed.

  1. Don’t have Shizuku yet? Follow the NightKeys guide’s step 1 to install it, then step 2, parts A–C to pair it. You only pair once.
  2. Open Shizuku and tap Start (under Start via Wireless debugging).
  3. Wait a few seconds until Shizuku says Running.

You should now see: Shizuku saying Running at the top.

Shizuku switches off every time your phone restarts. Until you start it again, your keyboard just works normally, without symbols on hold. Turn on Wi-Fi, open Shizuku and tap Start.

Next: step 2 ↓
02

Allow Hold Symbols

It needs two permissions: Shizuku, and Android’s accessibility setting so it can tell which key you’re holding. Android locks that second one for apps you download yourself, so the first time there’s an extra unlock.

  1. Open Titan Hold Symbols and tap Authorize / reconnect Shizuku. Tap Allow all the time.
  2. Tap Open accessibility settings and find Titan Hold Symbols in the list.
  3. Greyed out, with Controlled by Restricted Setting underneath? That’s normal for a downloaded app. Tap it once, then tap OK.
  4. Go back to the app and tap App info / restricted settings. Tap ⋮ in the top corner, choose Allow restricted settings and confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
  5. Back in accessibility settings, open Titan Hold Symbols, switch it on and tap Allow.

You should now see: Shizuku: Ready in the app, and Titan Hold Symbols switched on in accessibility settings.

Next: step 3 ↓
03

Turn it on and try it

  1. In the app, switch on Enable hold symbols and tap Turn on.
  2. Tap the Try it here box and type a few words like you normally would.
  3. Now hold S. After a moment you’ll feel a tick, and a 4 appears.
  4. Using Key Mapper? Turn its accessibility service off, or the two apps will fight over the same keys.

You should now see: Ready · hold a letter in any text field at the top of the app. It works anywhere you type.

If symbols pop up when you didn’t want them, or you’re waiting too long for them, move the Hold time slider in the app. It starts at 400 ms.

Stuck? Quick fixes

Titan Hold Symbols is greyed out in accessibility settings

That’s Android’s restricted setting, which it puts on every downloaded app. Tap the greyed entry once, then open App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings. The option only appears after you’ve tried to switch the service on once. Then switch it on again.

Holding a key still types the letter, or repeats it

Open the app and check the line at the top. Accessibility service is off means step 2 isn’t finished. Shizuku is not connected usually means the phone restarted: start Shizuku again, then tap Authorize / reconnect Shizuku. Paused means the switch or the Quick Settings tile turned it off.

A symbol comes out wrong in my keyboard app

Some keyboard apps handle certain symbols their own way. Change that letter’s symbol in the app, or turn on Insert symbols directly. Only use that if you have to, because it skips your keyboard app and can replace a word that’s still underlined.

I want my normal keyboard back for a while

Add the Hold Symbols tile to Quick Settings and tap it to pause. Turn it back on from the app. Switching the accessibility service off works too.

Uninstalling

Just uninstall it. Hold Symbols doesn’t change any system settings, so there’s nothing to put back.
