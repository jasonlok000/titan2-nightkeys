Three rules.
No more fumbling.

Your phone already has a backlight. NightKeys gives it a better sense of when to use it.

01 / DARK

Keep the keys visible.

Below your chosen light threshold, the keyboard stays illuminated while you use the phone. No need to keep pressing keys.

02 / BRIGHT

Let daylight do its thing.

When the room gets bright enough, the keyboard light switches off. A small buffer keeps it from flickering near the boundary.

03 / SCREEN OFF

Lights out means lights out.

NightKeys explicitly turns the backlight off when the screen goes off. Ambient sensing pauses until you wake it again.
