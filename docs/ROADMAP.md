# Roadmap

This is a small device-specific tool, not a promise of a large suite.

First: collect community beta feedback, verify the newly signed APK on real devices, improve setup clarity, and preserve the known-good screen-off/wake behavior.

Next candidates: additional Elite firmware reports, translations, stronger recovery evidence, same-key update tests and measured battery overhead. A finite renewable timeout may reduce a failure consequence, but writing a setting is not proof of renewing the firmware timer; investigate separately before changing the algorithm.

Future tools belong in reviewed proposals with purpose, permissions, safety/recovery, source, license and a beginner guide. Their catalogue entry may link to a separate repo. Nothing beyond NightKeys is currently announced as available.
