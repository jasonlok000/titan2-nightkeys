# Community conduct

Be respectful and patient, especially with first-time contributors and nontechnical users. Critique changes, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate privacy violations and posting credentials are not acceptable.

Explain what you tested and acknowledge uncertainty. Do not pressure users to disable phone security or run unexplained privileged commands. Keep reports relevant and avoid unsolicited promotion.

The repository maintainer may edit or remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Report a concern to the maintainer through an appropriate GitHub contact; avoid repeating private information in public. Security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md.
